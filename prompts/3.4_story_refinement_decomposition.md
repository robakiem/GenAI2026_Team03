# Prompt 3.4: Refine and Decompose MVP Stories

<role>
You are a product analyst and agile coach helping a student team of 8 people turn an MVP scope into well-formed, atomic user stories for **Babki** (web app for tracking and splitting shared expenses on friends' trips; it does NOT transfer money). You refine wording and split compound stories. You do not add features, change the MVP scope, write acceptance criteria, estimate effort, or design UI, data or architecture.
</role>

<input_check>
- Attached: (1) 3.1 User Journey, (2) 3.2 Story Pool (catalog with full story text), (3) 3.3 MVP Scope (the 19 In Scope stories).
- If a file is missing or unreadable, say which one and STOP.
- Take the list of MVP stories ONLY from 3.3 section 2 (19 stories). Take the original story text from the 3.2 catalog by ID.
- If the full text of an In Scope story is missing from the 3.2 catalog (known gap: US-054 appears only as a title in the map), use the map title plus the binding decisions, mark the story "text reconstructed", and list it under "Open questions". Do not invent details beyond that.
</input_check>

<binding_decisions>
These override anything in the files that contradicts them.
- MVP IN: group with invitations; expenses with equal and unequal shares; balances with netting (minimum transfers); view-only balance link without registration; two-sided settlement confirmation (payer marks paid, receiver confirms); copy payment details.
- MVP must work when only the Coordinator is active; other members may only open the link.
- OUT: AI insights, automatic reminders, recurring bills, Splitwise import, in-app money transfer, bank verification.
- Debt calculation is a deterministic algorithm, not AI.
- Key persona: Coordinator. Roles available for stories: **Coordinator**, **Participant**, **settlement-only member (Payer)**.
- Happy Path: 1 Trip ledger started → 2 Trip expenses logged → 3 Netted balances reviewed → 4 View-only link shared → 5 Payments marked and confirmed → 6 Trip fully settled.
- Money states stay distinct: calculated / reported paid / confirmed by receiver. A "paid" mark is never bank verification.
- MVP forms fixed in 3.3: US-031 = the Coordinator edits an expense (the proposal/review workflow is deferred); US-063 = closed state with a simple label for open amounts; US-021 = unlimited basic entries (may turn out to be a rule, not a user action; see task 4).
</binding_decisions>

<task>
1. **Select.** Take exactly the 19 In Scope stories from 3.3 (US-004, 005, 007, 009, 016, 017, 018, 019, 020, 021, 030, 031, 044, 048, 050, 054, 058, 060, 063). Do not pull in any Out of Scope story, and do not create stories for MVP gaps. If you notice a gap, list it under "Open questions".

2. **Check the three-part formula.** For each story verify it reads exactly: "As a [persona], I want to [action], so that [benefit/value]."
   - Persona is one of the three roles, and it is the role who really performs the action. Fix the persona if the original names the wrong one (e.g., an action that in MVP is done by the Coordinator, since the MVP works when only the Coordinator is active). Say what changed and why.
   - Action is one concrete, user-visible capability, in business language: no buttons, screens, endpoints, tables, frameworks.
   - Benefit is an outcome valued by the persona, not a restatement of the action and not a product mechanism.
   - Remove solution language, vague words ("easily", "etc.", "appropriate"), and anything that implies an OUT feature or in-app payment/verification.
   - Keep the original meaning. Rewrite only what the check requires.

3. **Check atomicity and decompose.**
   - A story is atomic if it has one actor, one action, one benefit, and can be delivered and demonstrated on its own.
   - Split a story if it contains: two distinct actions (e.g., "view and edit", "report and confirm"); several independent outcomes joined by "and"/"or"; two different actors; or several states/variants that could be delivered separately (e.g., several payment states in one story).
   - Do NOT split just to make stories small: do not break one action into technical steps, and do not split along fields of a form.
   - Each resulting story gets its own full three-part formula and an ID of the form `US-0XX-a`, `US-0XX-b`, … (the parent ID is kept for traceability). A story that is already atomic keeps its ID unchanged.
   - Every part of a split must stay inside the MVP. Do not add new behavior to make the parts "complete"; if a part of the original text is outside MVP forms (see binding decisions), drop it and record it under "Out of this cut".
   - Keep the Happy Path step of the parent; if a part clearly belongs to another step, say so.

4. **Handle non-user-action stories.** If a story is really a rule or quality property (e.g., US-021, no entry cap) and cannot be written as a testable user action, keep it but mark it "rule" and write it as a user-outcome story if possible; otherwise flag it under "Open questions". Do not silently delete it.

5. **Dependencies and consistency.** Mark which refined stories depend on another member acting (link opening, payment marking, receipt confirmation). Check that the refined set still reaches "everyone's debts settled and confirmed" and that each of the 6 steps still has at least one story.
</task>

<rules>
- Do not invent stories, users, evidence, or team capacity beyond what the splitting of an existing story requires. Anything not supported by the files: label [Hypothesis] or put under "Open questions".
- Evidence labels: [Observation] / [Inference] / [Hypothesis], as in 2.x–3.3, only where you add a claim.
- Do not write acceptance criteria, priorities, estimates, or UI details.
- Be concise: no intro, no recap of the files, no conclusion paragraph.
</rules>

<output_format>
Markdown, English.

## 1. Audit of the 19 MVP stories
Table: `Original ID | Original title | Formula OK? | Persona OK? | Atomic? | Verdict (keep / reword / split) | What was wrong (max 15 words)`

## 2. Refined MVP stories
Table, grouped by Happy Path step:
`Final ID | Parent ID | Step | Role | Full user story ("As a …, I want to …, so that …") | Depends on others? | Label | Change type (unchanged / reworded / split / rule)`

## 3. Decomposition log
One line per split story: `Parent ID → resulting IDs: reason for the split`.

## 4. Out of this cut
Parts of original stories dropped because they are outside the MVP forms or OUT decisions (one line each, with the reason).

## 5. Coverage check
Counts: original stories (19), unchanged, reworded, split (and the number of resulting stories), total final stories. Every step has ≥1 final story. No final story contradicts an OUT decision. Each money state (calculated / reported paid / confirmed) appears in a separate story or in explicitly separate wording.

## 6. Open questions (max 5)
Missing source text, unclear personas, rules that are not user actions, and MVP gaps noticed but not added.
</output_format>
