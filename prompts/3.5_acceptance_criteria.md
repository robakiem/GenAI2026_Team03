<product_context>
**Segment:** friends on multi-day trips in Ukraine. Several people pay for housing, food, transport, activities; not every expense includes everyone; at the end the group needs a checkable list of "who pays whom". Repayment happens **outside** Babki (bank app, cash). Couples, families, housemates, recurring bills, and one-off events are out of scope.

**Roles:** **Coordinator** (key persona; keeps the ledger, may enter everything alone, usually the main receiver), **Participant** (the MVP does not depend on Participants registering or entering expenses), **settlement-only member (Payer)** (may never register; opens a link, sees what they owe, pays outside, marks it paid).

**Binding team decisions:**
- **F3:** Babki never moves money. Settlement = two-sided confirmation: the payer reports "paid", the receiver confirms receipt. There is a "copy payment details" action. A confirmation is a person's statement, never bank verification, and no text may suggest otherwise.
- **F4:** the MVP must work when only the Coordinator is active; others may only open a link. A view-only page without registration is part of the MVP.
- **F6:** debt calculation is a deterministic algorithm, not AI.

**Happy Path:** 1 Trip ledger started → 2 Trip expenses logged → 3 Netted balances reviewed → 4 Link shared → 5 Payments marked and confirmed → 6 Trip fully settled.

**Three money states, never mixed:** **Open** (calculated) → **Reported paid** (the payer's claim) → **Confirmed by receiver**. "Reported paid" is not "money received". "Confirmed by receiver" is not "verified by the bank". An expense share is not the same as a netted repayment obligation.
</product_context>

<working_decisions>
The team has not yet settled some questions left open earlier. To keep criteria testable, apply these defaults. They are **proposed defaults that require team approval**. Label every criterion that depends on one with its ID (e.g., "per D2"). Do not invent other defaults silently, and do not offer "either/or" criteria where a default exists.

**D1. Personal link.** Each ledger member (registered or not) has one unguessable personal link. It works as both the invitation (US-007) and the member's page (US-009). The page shows only that member's own obligations ("you pay"), the transfers they should receive ("you receive"), the expenses behind their amounts (US-030), and the payment details of the people they must pay. It never shows other members' obligations. *(An earlier note mentioned one link for the group chat; a single shared link would expose everyone's balances, so personal links are assumed.)*

**D2. What a personal link can and cannot do.** The page is view-only for the ledger itself: it can never add, edit, or delete expenses, members, or payment details. The only actions it allows are: mark one's own outgoing transfer as Reported paid (US-054), and confirm one's own incoming transfer (US-058). Nobody can act on a transfer that does not involve them.

**D3. Payment details.** A receiver's payment details are free text of up to 100 characters (e.g., a card number as typed). In the MVP the Coordinator enters them for any member. Copying gives the text exactly as entered. If details are missing, the copy action is unavailable and the page says details were not provided. *(No story covers entering details: mark it "PROPOSED STORY, needs team approval" in Sections A and F, and write its criteria inside US-050.)*

**D4. Money rules.**
- UAH only; at most 2 decimals; minimum 0.01, maximum 999999.99 per expense.
- The sum of an expense's shares equals its amount exactly. Specific amounts that do not add up to the expense amount, or percentages that do not add up to exactly 100.00 %, are refused: the expense is not saved and the Coordinator sees the difference.
- Remainder rule: when an amount cannot be split evenly in kopecks, the remainder is given one kopeck at a time to participants in the order they were added to the ledger (100.00 among 3 gives 33.34, 33.33, 33.33). Percentage splits use the same remainder rule.
- An expense that would give any participant a 0.00 share is refused.
- Percentages: greater than 0, up to 2 decimals.

**D5. Balances and transfers.**
- Balance = what the member paid minus their shares across all expenses. All balances sum to exactly 0.00.
- The transfer list comes from a deterministic algorithm: the same expenses always give the same list, regardless of entry order. Every transfer shows payer, receiver, and amount.
- Carrying out all proposed transfers brings every balance to 0.00.
- The number of transfers never exceeds (members with non-zero balance − 1). Members with 0.00 appear in no transfer.
- Test this upper bound and determinism. Do not claim the list is the mathematical minimum and do not require one specific list when several valid lists exist, unless the team confirms a tie-breaking rule.

**D6. Transfer statuses: exactly three.** Open → Reported paid → Confirmed by receiver. Moves only forward. A confirmed transfer cannot be reverted. Confirming a transfer that is still Open is not allowed. A payer cannot confirm their own transfer. No text says "verified", "checked by the bank", or suggests Babki moved money.

**D7. Settlement started = expenses frozen.** As soon as at least one transfer is Reported paid or Confirmed, the Coordinator can no longer add or edit expenses. The attempt is refused with a message that explains why, and nothing changes. Before that moment, adding or editing expenses recalculates the proposed transfers immediately (US-048). *(Simplest safe rule. Alternative for the team: allow edits with a warning.)*

**D8. Closing the trip.** The Coordinator can close the trip at any time (a closing action, plus a derived summary). A closed trip shows the label "Closed", is read-only for expenses, and keeps personal links working for marking and confirming. Every transfer keeps its own status. Transfers that are not Confirmed are also grouped under a heading "Not confirmed" so they cannot be mistaken for repaid. The text "fully settled" appears only when every transfer is Confirmed by receiver. Closing twice changes nothing.

**D9. Test limits (test values, not performance promises).** No cap on expenses; demonstrate with 500 expenses and up to 20 members. The 21st member is refused with a message. Ledger name 1–60 characters. Member name 1–40 characters, unique in a ledger (case-insensitive). Do not invent other performance numbers.

**D10. Interface.** Ukrainian UI; usable in a mobile browser at 360 px width without horizontal scrolling and without installation. No in-app payment, bank connection, or reminder sending anywhere.

**D11. Coordinator acts for others (needed for F4).** So the Happy Path works when only the Coordinator is active, the Coordinator can mark any transfer as Reported paid on behalf of the payer (the page shows it was reported by the Coordinator), and can confirm transfers where the Coordinator is the receiver. The Coordinator cannot confirm transfers that another member should receive. *(PROPOSAL.)*

**D12. Repeated actions.** Marking an already Reported paid transfer as paid, confirming an already Confirmed transfer, or submitting the same action twice changes nothing and creates no duplicate; the user sees the current status.
</working_decisions>
