## 3. Decomposition log

- US-020 → US-020-a, US-020-b: "amounts or percentages" are two input variants that can be delivered separately.
- US-060 → US-060-a, US-060-b: two independent outcomes (knowing who hasn't paid; not mistaking a claim for received money). The partial and disputed states are dropped.

## 4. Out of this cut

- US-031: friends proposing corrections and the review workflow. The 3.3 MVP form is direct editing by the Coordinator.
- US-031: "without silently replacing the original" (correction history is US-032, deferred).
- US-060: "partially reported" state (depends on US-055, deferred).
- US-060: "disputed" state (depends on US-059, deferred).
- US-063: "disputed" amounts (US-062 deferred). Only "not yet confirmed" is labeled.
- US-048: "provided recipients receive the updated proposal". No notification is in MVP.
- US-007: "mobile-friendly" is treated as a quality property, not part of the story.

## 6. Open questions

1. **Receivers who are not the Coordinator.** In US-058, who confirms receipt if the receiver never registers, and who enters their payment details for US-050? This is an MVP gap, not added here.
2. **Rules vs actions.** Does US-021 need explicit work, or is it a non-functional rule? Is US-063 a real "close" action or only a derived "all confirmed" status? Are percentages (US-020-b) required, or are fixed amounts enough?
3. **Overlap and an unhandled case.** US-009 and US-030 overlap on the personal view. Also, no story says what happens when an expense is corrected (US-031/048) after a transfer was already marked paid or confirmed.
