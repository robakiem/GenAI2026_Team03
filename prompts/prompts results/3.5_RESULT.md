# Babki: Acceptance Criteria (MVP)

This document gives acceptance criteria for all 21 refined MVP user stories. Each story has (1) an exhaustive business-rule checklist and (2) Gherkin Given-When-Then scenarios: one Happy Path and the most important edge cases and errors. The checklist is the complete list of conditions; the scenarios illustrate the key ones. Rules marked "proposed default" (D1–D12) are working decisions that the team must confirm; scenarios that depend on them are tagged `@conditional`.

Money states used everywhere: `Open` (calculated) → `Reported paid` (the payer's statement) → `Confirmed by receiver` (the receiver's statement). Babki never moves money and never verifies bank transactions.

### A2. Transfer status transitions

| Current status | Event | Who can do it | New status | Forbidden / note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open | Report payment | The payer through their personal link; the Coordinator on the payer's behalf (D11) | Reported paid | Does not mean the receiver has the money |
| Reported paid | Confirm receipt | The receiver through their personal link; the Coordinator if the Coordinator is the receiver (D11) | Confirmed by receiver | Does not mean bank verification |
| Open | Confirm receipt | Nobody | Open | Refused |
| Reported paid | Report payment again | Same people as above | Reported paid | Changes nothing (BR-22) |
| Confirmed by receiver | Report or confirm again | Nobody | Confirmed by receiver | Changes nothing; never reverted |
| Any | A payer confirms their own outgoing transfer | Payer | Unchanged | Refused |
| Any | The Coordinator confirms a transfer that another member should receive | Coordinator | Unchanged | Refused |

### A3. Verified example data

Members were added in this order: Olia (Coordinator), Max, Iryna, Andrii. Ledger "Carpathians". All amounts in UAH.

| Expense | Paid by | Olia | Max | Iryna | Andrii | Total shares |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| House rent 6000.00 (equal, all four) | Olia | 1500.00 | 1500.00 | 1500.00 | 1500.00 | 6000.00 |
| Groceries 1850.50 (equal, all four) | Max | 462.63 | 462.63 | 462.62 | 462.62 | 1850.50 |
| Taxi 420.00 (equal, Olia, Iryna, Andrii) | Iryna | 140.00 | 0.00 | 140.00 | 140.00 | 420.00 |
| Funicular 900.00 (specific amounts) | Andrii | 250.00 | 250.00 | 250.00 | 150.00 | 900.00 |
| Total shares | | 2352.63 | 2212.63 | 2352.62 | 2252.62 | 9170.50 |

Groceries: 1850.50 / 4 = 462.625, so each gets 462.62 (1850.48 in total) and the remaining 0.02 goes to Olia and Max in ledger order.

| Member | Paid | Shares | Balance (paid − shares) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Olia | 6000.00 | 2352.63 | +3647.37 |
| Max | 1850.50 | 2212.63 | −362.13 |
| Iryna | 420.00 | 2352.62 | −1932.62 |
| Andrii | 900.00 | 2252.62 | −1352.62 |
| Total | 9170.50 | 9170.50 | 0.00 |

| Proposed transfer | Amount |
|---|---|
| Max → Olia | 362.13 |
| Iryna → Olia | 1932.62 |
| Andrii → Olia | 1352.62 |
| Total | 3647.37 |

Three transfers for four members (3 = 4 − 1) meet the upper bound of BR-14; the total equals Olia's positive balance. Arithmetic status: verified.

#### US-004: Create a named trip ledger

**User story:** As a Coordinator, I want to create a named ledger for our trip, so that all shared expenses of this trip are kept in one place, separate from other spending.

**Meta:** Step 1 | Coordinator | Depends on others: No | Rules: BR-01, BR-25, BR-26 | Decisions: D9, D10 | Type: user action

**Business-rule checklist**

- [ ] `AC-US-004-01` (Happy; story) The Coordinator can create a ledger by giving it a name.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-02` (Rule; BR-26) A new ledger has 0 expenses and every balance is 0.00 UAH.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-03` (Rule; D9) Two ledgers may have the same name and remain separate ledgers.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-04` (Permission; story) The person who creates the ledger is its Coordinator.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-05` (Error; BR-01) An empty name or a name with only spaces is refused and no ledger is created.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-06` (Error; BR-01) A name of 61 characters is refused and no ledger is created.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-07` (Edge; BR-01) Names of exactly 1 and exactly 60 characters are accepted.
- [ ] `AC-US-004-08` (Rule; BR-25) Creating a ledger works in a mobile browser at 360 px width without horizontal scrolling or installation.
