## 5. Risks of the cut

1. **No plain "edit expense" story in the pool.** US-031 is a proposal workflow; MVP takes it as "the Coordinator edits". Needs clarification in 3.4.
2. **Rounding without explanation.** The algorithm must still split cents (US-047 deferred); otherwise totals differ by 1–2 UAH and mistrust grows [Hypothesis].
3. **Two-sided confirmation relies on others (US-054, US-058).** If friends only open the link and nobody marks payment, transfers never reach "confirmed" and step 6 stalls.
4. **Netting may be unwelcome (US-046 deferred).** Groups that dislike simplified settlements have no alternative plan [Critical: A11].
5. **Link privacy (US-006 deferred).** The view-only link exposes financial data; without access rules it must at least be unguessable.

## 6. Open questions

5. Is a Ukrainian-language interface mandatory in MVP (US-042 deferred as a separate feature)?
