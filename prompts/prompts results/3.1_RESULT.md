## 1. Key persona

**Choice: Coordinator.** The friend on a multi-day trip who keeps the ledger, compiles the totals and tells the others what they owe.

- The Coordinator carries most of the pain: the daily entry cap, re-compiling totals from chats and receipts, and chasing repayments [VPC: "How the roles differ"; Pains P1, P2, P5, all Role = Coord]. They are also the one hypothesized to start using a tool in the first place [Audience: Key profile 1, "also initiates tool adoption", Hypothesis].
- It is the only role that gets value on its own: "Babki must first work in a 'Coordinator-only + read-only links' mode" [VPC: "How the three roles interact"]. The most fragile step in the adoption chain is "others join and keep logging" [Critical: sections 5–6, F4], so a journey that depends on any other role would rest on that weakest assumption.
- Of the three candidates, its existence has the highest confidence, rated medium [Audience: Key profile 1]. For Ukraine it is still a Hypothesis [Critical: re-ratings, "Coordinator role … → HYPOTHESIS for Ukraine"].

**Rejected candidates**
- **Participant:** delivers value only if they keep logging during the trip, which is the riskiest assumption (A7) [Critical: F4]. The profile's evidence also comes from household settings, not trips [Audience: Key profile 2].
- **Payer:** lowest confidence. "Do not treat it as a validated separate segment" [Audience: Key profile 3]. They have no reason to register, because bank requests reach them for free [VPC: "How the roles differ"].

**Anti-persona**
- The person who paid for a one-off dinner or gift and wants to be paid back once. Privat24 and monobank already do this job with real money movement [VPC: Segment 4, "No target"].
- Couples or families with a shared fund, for whom an exact debt ledger contradicts how they manage money [VPC: Segment 3b]. The same goes for all-monobank groups, where Babki is "strictly weaker" [VPC: Segment 1, Fit].

## 3. Intersections with other roles

- Step 1 (Coordinator) → Participant / Payer: receives an invitation to the trip group and can join or ignore it without blocking the journey.
- Step 2 (Coordinator) → Participant: if they joined, sees the expenses logged so far and may add their own. Payer: sees nothing until step 4.
- Step 2 (Participant, optional) → Coordinator: expenses added by others appear in the same ledger and balances.
- Step 4 (Coordinator) → Payer / Participant: opens the link without registering and sees their amount, the expenses behind it, whom to pay, and the receiver's payment details to copy.
- Step 5 (Payer / Participant) → Coordinator, or another receiver: sees the debt marked paid and waiting for their confirmation. After the receiver confirms, the payer sees the debt closed.
- Step 6 (Coordinator) → all members: anyone who opens the link sees that the trip is fully settled.

## 4. Deferred deviations

1. A member disputes their share or the amount of an expense after seeing the link.
2. A debtor marks a debt as paid, but the receiver says the money never arrived.
3. A member never opens the link, or opens it and never pays.
4. An expense is added or corrected after the link with the final balances has been shared.
5. A debtor pays only part of what they owe.

## 5. Open questions (max 5)

1. **Marking a debt paid from the view-only link.** Can a debtor who has only the link, without registering, mark a debt as paid, or does the Coordinator mark it for them? Two-sided confirmation and "view-only without registration" pull in opposite directions. [Hypothesis]
2. **Who enters receivers' payment details.** Who adds the payment details for receivers who never register: the receiver or the Coordinator on their behalf? Consent and privacy for other members' financial data are UNKNOWN [Critical: section 7].
3. **Chasing slow payers without reminders.** In step 5 it seemed that automatic reminders were needed to chase slow payers, but they are OUT of scope and were left out. The journey assumes debtors act on the link after a manual ask in the chat. Whether that is enough is untested [VPC: J3, P5; Critical: F3, section 7]. [Hypothesis]
4. **Coordinator-only mode.** Will one Coordinator really log a whole trip alone and get paid back when others only open the link (A7–A9)? Does this save them effort compared with a spreadsheet [Critical: F4, section 8]? It is also unclear whether the trigger comes at the start of the trip or only at the end [Audience: Key profile 1, trigger is a Hypothesis]. If it comes at the end, step 2 becomes reconstruction after the trip rather than logging during it.
5. **Do groups want the minimum number of transfers?** One trip complaint concerns simplified settlements, so A11 is weakly CONTRADICTED [Critical: section 0; Audience: trips row]. Step 3 assumes groups accept a netted list over direct paybacks. [Hypothesis]
