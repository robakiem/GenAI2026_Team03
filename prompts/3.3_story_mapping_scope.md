# Prompt 3.3: Story Map and MVP Scope Boxing

<!-- Repository metadata
Purpose: arrange the story pool along the Happy Path and draw the MVP / Post-MVP line
Input files: (1) 3.1 User Journey RESULT, (2) 3.2 Story Pool and Map RESULT
Model / date of run: Claude Sonnet 5.5 / 2026-10-14

<role>
You are a product analyst helping a student team of 8 people fix a realistic MVP scope for **Babki** (web app for tracking and splitting shared expenses on friends' trips; it does NOT transfer money). The MVP must be coded and demonstrated live by the end of the course. You do not write new stories, acceptance criteria, UI or architecture.
</role>

<input_check>
- Attached: (1) 3.1 User Journey, (2) 3.2 Story Pool (IDs US-001…US-069, map in section C, catalog in section D).
- If a file is missing or unreadable, say which one and STOP.
- Story text in the 3.2 catalog may be incomplete for some IDs (e.g., US-053…US-057 appear only as titles in the map). Use the map title, and flag the gap.
</input_check>

<binding_decisions>
These come from the team after 3.1 and override anything in the files:
- MVP IN: group with invitations; expenses with equal and unequal shares; balances with netting (minimum transfers); view-only balance link without registration; two-sided settlement confirmation (payer marks paid, receiver confirms); copy payment details.
- MVP must work when only the Coordinator is active.
- OUT: AI insights, automatic reminders, recurring bills, Splitwise import, in-app money transfer, bank verification.
- Debt calculation is a deterministic algorithm, not AI.
- Key persona: Coordinator. Happy Path: 1 Trip ledger started → 2 Trip expenses logged → 3 Netted balances reviewed → 4 View-only link shared → 5 Payments marked and confirmed → 6 Trip fully settled.
</binding_decisions>

<task>
1. **Story map.** Place every story US-001…US-069 under exactly one of the 6 Happy Path steps (the backbone). Stories that fit no step go to a separate column "Outside the Happy Path" (branches, post-trip, edge cases). Do not create new stories.

2. **Cut line.** Classify every story as **In Scope (MVP)** or **Out of Scope (Post-MVP)**.
   Use these criteria in order:
   - (a) Required for the Happy Path to reach "everyone's debts settled and confirmed", or explicitly named in the binding decisions → In.
   - (b) Without it the Coordinator-only mode breaks or the result is untrustworthy (e.g., wrong money states) → In.
   - (c) Everything else, including "nice to have", exploratory, privacy/export/reuse ideas and anything conflicting with OUT decisions → Out.
   Keep MVP the **smallest end-to-end slice**: each of the 6 steps must have at least one In story; prefer one simple story over several variants.

3. **Justify.** For each In story: one line "why it cannot wait", citing criterion (a) or (b) and the Happy Path step. For each Out story: one line "why it can wait" and what the team loses by deferring it (max 10 words). Group the Out list by reason (e.g., depends on others' activity, out-of-scope feature, low evidence, polish).

4. **Risk check.** Name up to 5 risks of this cut: stories deferred that the MVP implicitly depends on, In stories that rely on other members acting, and In stories that are large to build. Do not redesign; just name them.
</task>

<rules>
- Do not invent stories, IDs, evidence or team capacity. If something is unknown, write [Hypothesis] or ask under "Open questions".
- Evidence labels: [Observation] / [Inference] / [Hypothesis], as in 2.x–3.2.
- Money states must stay distinct: calculated / reported paid / confirmed by receiver. A "paid" mark is never bank verification.
- Be concise: no intro, no recap of the files, no conclusion paragraph.
</rules>

<output_format>
Markdown, English.

## 1. Story map
Table: `Happy Path step | Story IDs and short titles` (rows: steps 1–6 + "Outside the Happy Path").

## 2. MVP scope (In)
Table: `ID | Short title | Step | Why it cannot wait`

## 3. Post-MVP (Out)
Table grouped by reason: `ID | Short title | Why it can wait | Cost of deferring`

## 4. Coverage check
Counts: total, In, Out (must sum to 69); every step has ≥1 In story; no In story contradicts an OUT decision.

## 5. Risks of the cut (max 5)
## 6. Open questions (max 5)
</output_format>
